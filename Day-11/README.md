# Day 11 – Filtering, Sorting & Exploring Data

## Task

Applied filters and sorting to explore the Employee Details dataset and identify the Top 5 and Bottom 5 performers based on Performance Rating.

## Work Completed

- Applied a filter to the Employee Details dataset.
- Excluded `Unknown` values from the Performance Rating filter.
- Sorted Performance Rating from Largest to Smallest to identify the Top 5 performers.
- Sorted Performance Rating from Smallest to Largest to identify the Bottom 5 performers.
- Created a mini report containing the Top 5 and Bottom 5 performer records.
- Documented key patterns observed from the filtered and sorted data.

## Top 5 Performers

The Top 5 records were identified from employees with a Performance Rating of 5.

## Bottom 5 Performers

The Bottom 5 records were identified from employees with a Performance Rating of 1.

## Key Patterns

1. The Top 5 performers all have a Performance Rating of 5.
2. The Bottom 5 performers all have a Performance Rating of 1.
3. Performance Rating values range from 1 to 5 among the numeric ratings.
4. Some employee records contain `Unknown` values in fields such as Department, Country, Salary, Gender, or Email.

## Conclusion

The Day 11 exercise helped me practice filtering, sorting, and exploring employee data to identify top and bottom performance records and summarize key patterns.
